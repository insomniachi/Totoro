libvlc (VLC media player's engine), included unmodified in this folder, from the VideoLAN.LibVLC.Windows 3.0.24
package. It plays videos in Totoro's built-in player, and songs' audio.

https://download.videolan.org/pub/videolan/vlc/3.0.24/.

You may replace these files with your own build of libvlc 3.0.x: Totoro loads them from this folder.
